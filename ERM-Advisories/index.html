ERM Advisory  ·  AI Risk Series

Control expectations for GenAI and agentic AI in business processes

Thirteen advisories setting out what Enterprise Risk Management expects of the key controls where the firm integrates or augments its business processes with generative and agentic AI.

This series addresses the position of an adopter of AI capability — a firm embedding third-party generative and agentic AI into existing processes — rather than a developer of foundation models. Each advisory follows the same structure: control objective, principles, seven minimum standards, the circumstances in which the control alone is insufficient, roles across the three lines, and a short self-check for use-case owners.

The advisories are interdependent. Registration (002) determines which of the others apply; identity (006) bounds the consequence of injection (008); logging (009) is what makes every other control testable.

How to use this series: start with ERM-ADV-AI-002 to register and tier the use-case. The tier determines which further advisories apply. Advisories 006, 007 and 008 apply only where the AI is given access to systems or permitted to take actions.
