What this assessment is: a structured first-line self-assessment producing a draft view of control maturity for one use-case. It is not a risk rating, a control effectiveness conclusion, or an approval. Second-line challenge and evidence testing sit outside this tool. A high score based on assertion rather than evidence is exactly the outcome the advisories are written to prevent.
